Description:

Bánh Xèo (Vietnamese sizzling crepe) is a beloved, crispy street food classic named after the loud sizzling sound (xèo) the batter makes when poured into a hot skillet. It is an interactive, finger-food dish celebrated for its contrast between a hot, shattering exterior and fresh, cool herbs.

Ingredients:

  • 1 (10-inch) fresh, airy Vietnamese baguette (or French baguette)
  • 2 tablespoons pork liver pâté
  • 1.5 tablespoons Vietnamese mayonnaise (egg butter)
  • 3 ounces Vietnamese cold cuts (such as chả lụa, jambon, or headcheese)
  • 4 thin, lengthwise slices of fresh cucumber
  • ¼ cup pickled daikon and carrots (đồ chua)
  • 5 sprigs of fresh cilantro
  • 3 thin diagonal slices of fresh jalapeño or bird's eye chili
  • 1 teaspoon Maggi seasoning sauce (or premium soy sauce)

Steps:

  1. Whisk the batter: Mix rice flour, turmeric, a pinch of salt, coconut milk, and water until smooth. Let it rest for 15-30 minutes, then stir in chopped green onions.
  2. Sear the proteins: Heat a splash of oil in a hot skillet. Sauté a few pieces of pork and shrimp until mostly cooked through.
  3. Sizzle the crepe: Pour a thin ladle of batter into the pan, swirling it quickly to coat the bottom completely. It should make a loud xèo sound.
  4. Steam the veggies: Drop cooked mung beans and a handful of bean sprouts onto one half of the crepe. Cover with a lid for 1 to 2 minutes to steam the sprouts.
  5. Crisp it up: Remove the lid, drizzle a little oil around the edges, and cook uncovered for another few minutes until the bottom turns golden brown and shattering-crisp.
  6. Fold and serve: Fold the crepe in half over the veggies. Slide it onto a plate and serve immediately with lettuce, herbs, and warm dipping sauce.
