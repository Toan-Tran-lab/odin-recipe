Description:

Banh Mi is a world-renowned Vietnamese sandwich that perfectly embodies culinary fusion, combining a crispy French-style baguette with vibrant, savory, and tangy Vietnamese ingredients. It is celebrated globally for its incredible contrast of textures and balanced, explosive flavors.

Ingredients:

  • 1 cup (120 grams) white rice flour
  • 1 tablespoon cornstarch
  • ½ teaspoon ground turmeric powder
  • ½ teaspoon sea salt
  • ¾ cup (180 ml) creamy coconut milk
  • ¾ cup (180 ml) ice-cold water (or carbonated club soda for extra crispness)
  • 3 green onions, thinly chopped
  • ½ pound pork belly, sliced into paper-thin, bite-sized strips
  • ½ pound medium shrimp, peeled and deveined
  • 1.5 cups fresh mung bean sprouts
  • ¼ cup split mung beans, steamed until soft
  • 2 tablespoons vegetable oil (divided for frying)
  • 1 head of green leaf lettuce or mustard greens (for wrapping)
  • 1 bunch of mixed fresh herbs (mint, Thai basil, and perilla)
  • ½ cup Nước Chấm (Vietnamese sweet and tangy dipping sauce)

Steps:

  1. Prep the baguette: Slice a fresh, crispy baguette lengthwise, leaving one side attached like a jacket. Toast it briefly so the crust is ultra-crisp and the inside is warm.
  2. Spread the base: Slather a generous layer of pâté on one side of the bread and Vietnamese mayonnaise (egg butter) on the other.
  3. Layer the proteins: Add your meats—such as Vietnamese cold cuts, grilled pork, or warm chicken—directly over the pâté.
  4. Tuck in the crunch: Lay down thin, long slices of fresh cucumber to create a refreshing barrier.
  5. Add the tang: Stuff in a handful of crunchy pickled daikon and carrots (đồ chua).
  6. Finish with aromatics: Top it off with fresh cilantro sprigs, a few chili slices (if you like it spicy), and a quick drizzle of Maggi seasoning sauce or soy sauce.
