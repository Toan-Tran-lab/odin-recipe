Vietnamese Recipe: Pho

Description:

Phở is the iconic, globally celebrated national dish of Vietnam, consisting of a deeply aromatic, slow-simmered broth, flat rice noodles (bánh phở), tender meat, and an array of fresh, vibrant herbs. Revered as the ultimate comfort food, it is a masterful balance of rich savory flavors, comforting warmth, and clean, uplifting freshness.

Ingredients:

  • 5 pounds beef marrow and knuckle bones
  • 2 pounds beef brisket
  • 1 pound eye of round or sirloin steak, sliced paper-thin
  • 16 cups (4 liters) water
  • 2 large yellow onions, halved
  • 1 (4-inch) piece fresh ginger, halved lengthwise
  • 5 whole star anise pods
  • 1 whole cinnamon stick
  • 4 whole cloves
  • 1 tablespoon coriander seeds
  • ¼ cup premium fish sauce
  • 1.5 tablespoons yellow rock sugar
  • 1 tablespoon sea salt
  • 1.5 pounds fresh flat rice noodles (bánh phở)
  • Garnishes (to taste): Mung bean sprouts, fresh cilantro, Thai basil, scallions, and thin chili slices.

Steps:

  1. Parboil the bones: Place the beef bones in a large pot, cover with cold water, and boil hard for 5 minutes. Drain the water and rinse the bones thoroughly under cold water to remove impurities. This ensures a crystal-clear broth.
  2. Char the aromatics: Place the halved onion and sliced ginger directly over a gas flame or under a broiler until they are slightly blackened and fragrant.
  3. Toast the spices: In a dry pan over medium heat, toast the star anise, cinnamon stick, and cloves for 2 to 3 minutes until you smell their aroma.
  4. Simmer the broth: Return the clean bones to the pot. Add 3 quarts of fresh water, the charred onion and ginger, and the toasted spices. Bring to a boil, then lower the heat to a gentle simmer. Simmer uncovered for at least 2 to 3 hours, skimming any foam off the top.
  5. Season the soup: Strain the broth through a fine mesh sieve into a clean pot, discarding the solids. Stir in the fish sauce, sugar, and salt. Taste and adjust to your liking—it should taste bold and savory.
  6. Prep the noodles: Cook the rice noodles according to the package instructions, drain well, and divide them into serving bowls.
  7. Assemble and serve: Lay the raw, thinly sliced beef and chopped scallions directly on top of the hot noodles. Ladle the boiling hot broth immediately over the beef to cook it instantly.
